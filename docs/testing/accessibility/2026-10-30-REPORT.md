## Defects for Dwijesh

1. **Persistent countdown (SRS §3.1).** `.timer` scrolls out of the viewport.
   At page bottom its bottom edge was -937.5px on desktop and about -1003px on
   both mobile widths. The student must scroll back to see remaining time.
   [Desktop bottom](2026-10-30-verified/exam-bottom-1440.png),
   [390px bottom](2026-10-30-verified/exam-bottom-390.png),
   [320px bottom](2026-10-30-verified/exam-bottom-320.png).
   Keep a readable countdown visible during answering at every scroll position,
   without covering questions/options; retest keyboard focus and mobile reflow.
2. **Contrast (OEP-NF-005 / WCAG 1.4.3).** Lighthouse reports footer text
   `#788777` on `#f5f6f2` at **3.49:1** (required 4.5:1 for this small text).
   Desktop's active navigation number `01`, `#52734e` on `#e9edcf`, measures
   **4.45:1**. Raw reports contain failing selectors and element snippets.
3. **Additional mobile observation.** Lighthouse's WCAG **2.2** target-size rule
   flags the 45.5×16.5px Sign out target and its proximity to the brand link.
   This is useful to address, but is not misrepresented as an explicit WCAG 2.1
   acceptance requirement.

## Focused manual/keyboard observations and limits

The audit used real Tab/Enter navigation: the skip link is first in tab order
and moves focus to main. Each of 15 radio controls has a nonempty label and a
fieldset/legend group. An answer radio reached by Tab displayed a 3px outline
with 3px offset. Screenshots were visually inspected for readable question and
option layout at 390px. [Top of mobile exam](2026-10-30-verified/exam-top-390.png).

These limited observations are not a completed manual WCAG audit. Full
keyboard answering/clearing, save-error announcements, confirmation focus,
expiry/closed states, screen-reader behavior, zoom/forced-colors and focus-ring
contrast still need verification. The runner does not select answers or submit
the fixture. A score >=90 does not override the contrast defects. Retest against
the final integrated build after the owner fixes the UI.
